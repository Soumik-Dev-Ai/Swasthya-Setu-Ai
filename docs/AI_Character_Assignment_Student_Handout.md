## Build Your Own AI Character

Make a chatbot with its own personality — on your phone, at home, in about 40 minutes.

Due: 3rd August, 11:59 PM · Work on your own — this is an individual assignment.

## What you’re making

You’re going to build your own AI chatbot with a personality you design. A tutor, a coach, a storyteller, a chef — anything you like. You won’t write any code. You’ll just tell the AI who to be, in plain English, and it becomes that character.

Everything is done in your phone’s web browser using a free Google tool called AI Studio. No app to download, no laptop needed.

## Before you start

- A phone with internet — any phone with a browser works.

- A Google account — any Google account is fine — personal or school. If one account won’t let you into AI Studio, just try another.

- About 40 minutes — quiet time where you can type and test.

## Step by step

On your phone’s browser, go to aistudio.google.com and sign in with your Google account. Tap to start a new chat / prompt.

This is the box where you tell the AI who it should be. It’s labelled “Custom instructions” (it may sit behind a settings/gear icon). This is the most important box — everything happens here.

Copy the template below and fill in every blank with your own choices. Then paste your finished version into the Custom instructions box.

You are [NAME], a [WHAT YOU ARE].

Your personality is [THREE WORDS].

You always [ONE THING YOU ALWAYS DO].

You never [ONE THING YOU NEVER DO].

If someone asks something off-topic, you [WHAT YOU DO].

Now chat with your bot. Ask it a few things and see how it replies. Try three kinds of questions:

A normal question — something your character should easily answer.

A tricky question — something that tests one of your rules.

An off-topic question — something unrelated, to see if it stays in character.

## Worked example — “Chef Bikash”

Here’s the template filled in, so you can see what a finished one looks like:

You are Chef Bikash, a Bengali food expert.

Your personality is warm, funny, proud of Bengali cuisine.

You always suggest a real dish and explain it simply.

You never recommend food that isn’t Bengali.

If someone asks something off-topic, you gently bring them back to food.

Chatting with this bot, “What should I cook tonight?” might get a warm suggestion of shorshe ilish with a simple explanation. “Tell me a good pizza recipe” would get politely steered back to Bengali food. That’s the character’s rules working.

## Stuck? Quick fixes

- Bot ignores its personality — make your “personality” and “always” lines more specific and forceful.

- Bot breaks character on off-topic questions — strengthen your last line so it always steers back.

- Answers are boring — add a rule about how it should talk (short sentences, use examples, be funny).

- Can’t find the Custom instructions box — look under the settings or gear icon; it’s sometimes hidden there on phones.
